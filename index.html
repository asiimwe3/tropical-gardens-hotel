Western Uganda

Explore Kyenjojo & Western Uganda

Tropical Gardens Hotel is your base for discovering the best of Kyenjojo district and the western Uganda region.

Kyenjojo Town

A bustling town on the Hoima-Fort Portal highway, Kyenjojo is the commercial hub of Kyenjojo district. The hotel is centrally located for easy access to markets, banks, and local businesses.

Fort Portal (45 km)

The tourism capital of western Uganda, known for its cool climate, crater lakes, and proximity to Kibale National Park and Semuliki National Park. A 45-minute drive from the hotel.

Kibale National Park

One of Africa's best destinations for chimpanzee trekking, about 75 km from the hotel. Home to 13 primate species and over 300 bird species in a pristine tropical rainforest.

Rwenzori Mountains

The "Mountains of the Moon" offer spectacular hiking and mountaineering. Accessible via Fort Portal, these UNESCO World Heritage peaks are a must-see for adventure travellers.

Tea Estates

The rolling hills around Kyenjojo and Fort Portal are covered in lush tea plantations. Scenic drives and photography opportunities abound on the route to the hotel.

Kampala to Kyenjojo

A scenic 290 km drive (3-4 hours) on Hoima Road from the capital. The hotel offers free secure parking for guests driving from Kampala, Entebbe, or other parts of Uganda.

Guest Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Fast answers for guests searching for hotels in Kyenjojo, western Uganda accommodation, restaurant service, events, and secure booking.

Where is Tropical Gardens Hotel located?

Tropical Gardens Hotel is located at JJ9V+2V6 on Hoima Road in Kyenjojo, Uganda, with easy access for guests travelling through western Uganda.

How do I book a room?

Use the reservation form on this website, call reception, or send a WhatsApp message. The hotel team can confirm availability, room type, and arrival details.

Can I pay by Mobile Money or card?

Yes. Booking deposits can be paid through Pesapal checkout, which supports Mobile Money and card payment options where available.

Does the hotel have food, drinks, and events?

Yes. Tropical Gardens Hotel offers rooms, restaurant service, bar and drinks, gardens, conference space, weddings, meetings, and private functions.

Is reception available at night?

Reception support is available 24 hours. Guests can also use the direct call and WhatsApp links for arrival support, directions, and booking questions.

Is the hotel good for guests travelling from Kampala?

Yes. Kyenjojo is about 290 km from Kampala, a 3-4 hour drive on Hoima Road. Tropical Gardens Hotel is perfectly positioned for travellers heading to Fort Portal, Kibale National Park, and western Uganda. Free parking is available for all guests.

What are the room rates at Tropical Gardens Hotel Kyenjojo?

Our room rates start from UGX 80,000 for Standard Rooms, UGX 150,000 for Deluxe Rooms, and UGX 250,000 for Family Suites. All prices include free WiFi, free parking, and 24-hour reception service.

Does the hotel have WiFi and parking?

Yes. Free WiFi is available throughout the hotel, and we offer secure, free parking for all guests. These amenities are included in every room rate with no hidden charges.

Can I host a wedding or conference at Tropical Gardens Hotel?

Absolutely. Our conference hall accommodates up to 200 guests, and our garden events space is perfect for weddings, receptions, corporate retreats, and celebrations. Call +256 782 460683 to discuss packages and availability.

What tourist attractions are near Kyenjojo?

Kyenjojo is the gateway to western Uganda. Nearby attractions include Fort Portal (45 km), Kibale National Park for chimpanzee trekking, the Rwenzori Mountains, crater lakes, and scenic tea estates. Tropical Gardens Hotel is the ideal base for exploring the region.

Is Tropical Gardens Hotel suitable for business travellers?

Yes. We offer free WiFi, a conference hall for meetings, quiet and comfortable rooms, 24-hour reception, secure parking, and a restaurant serving meals throughout the day. Ideal for business travellers visiting Kyenjojo and western Uganda.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 10:00 AM. Our reception is open 24 hours for late arrivals. Contact us in advance if you need early check-in or late check-out arrangements.

How do I get to Tropical Gardens Hotel from Kampala or Entebbe?

The hotel is on Hoima Road in Kyenjojo, about 290 km from Kampala and 320 km from Entebbe Airport. You can drive (3-4 hours), take a bus to Kyenjojo town, or contact us to arrange pickup. The hotel is clearly marked on Google Maps.

Why is Tropical Gardens Hotel the best choice in Kyenjojo?

We offer the best combination of comfort, location, and value in Kyenjojo: 24-hour service, full restaurant and bar, conference facilities, garden events space, secure online booking with Mobile Money and card payments, free WiFi and parking, and a proven track record of guest satisfaction.
